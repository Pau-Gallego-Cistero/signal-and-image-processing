# 🎵 Mathematical Audio Processing

An exploration of signal processing applied to commercial music, built entirely in **Wolfram Mathematica**.

## Project Goal
This was a **self-directed project** born out of our own motivation and curiosity about the intersection of mathematics, physics, and music. 

Developed in collaboration with **Álvaro Ubierna**, the objective was to take a raw audio file (initially Radiohead's *"Let Down"*) and apply mathematical transformations to analyze its frequency spectrum, filter noise, and visualize its acoustic properties over time.

## Features & Methods
* **Waveform Extraction:** Imported and digitized stereo audio signals.
* **Fourier Transforms (FFT):** Converted time-domain signals into the frequency domain.
* **Filtering:** Applied mathematical filters to isolate and analyze specific frequency bands.
* **Spectrogram Visualization:** Generated heatmaps of frequency intensity over time.
